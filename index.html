OpenCITE: Free Scholarly Search for Citizen Scientists and Citizen Scholars

OpenCITE is a free meta-search tool for open-access scholarly research. Our mission is to bring the light of knowledge to the citizen scientist and the citizen scholar by removing barriers to peer-reviewed learning. We search DOAJ, OpenAlex, Crossref, Europeana, and Semantic Scholar in parallel — returning credible sources with MLA and APA citations ready to paste. No account required. No tracking. No paywall.
