OpenCITE is a meta-search over third-party open-access scholarly, library, museum and archive databases, available at citation.today as a free website and as a paid API and MCP server. We retrieve, deduplicate and relay records and format citations. We do not own the underlying records and do not guarantee their completeness or accuracy. Sources may be added, changed or removed at any time.

2. Web search and subscriptions

Searching on the website is free for up to 10 searches per day. Signing in with Google adds a saved library and search history synced to your account and gives you a monthly allowance of free API credits.

  • Student ($5/month) and Pro ($10/month) are subscriptions processed by Stripe, priced in US dollars, that renew monthly until cancelled. Both remove the daily web search limit and include a monthly API credit allowance (Student 500, Pro 1,000) that tops the balance up each calendar month and does not accumulate.
  • Manage your subscription yourself from Plans → Manage billing (a Stripe-hosted page): cancel, switch between Student and Pro, update your payment method or billing details, and download invoices and receipts.
  • Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep the plan until the end of the period you have paid for, then the account returns to the free plan. Upgrading from Student to Pro takes effect immediately and charges the prorated difference; downgrading from Pro to Student takes effect at the next renewal.
