Privacy Policy

Effective 28 September 2026 · Controller: Shahbaz Yusuf · contact

Searching without an account

You can search without signing in. Your query is sent from your browser directly to the upstream databases you have enabled (and, for a few sources, through our proxy so the upstream sees our server, not you). We do not store anonymous queries. Settings, library and history for anonymous use stay in your browser's local storage.

Processors

  • Vercel hosts the site and API; request logs (IP, path, status) are retained briefly. Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights collect aggregate, cookieless page metrics.
  • Supabase hosts the database.
  • Stripe processes payments and holds your billing details under its own privacy policy.
  • Google provides sign-in.
  • Upstream databases (for example OpenAlex, Crossref, DOAJ, PubMed, Europeana, DPLA, Smithsonian, Gallica, Internet Archive) receive the query text and, for API calls, our server's identity — never your account.
  • Ko-fi powers the optional donation widget under its own terms.

Cookies

One strictly necessary session cookie when you sign in. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

Retention and deletion

Account data is kept while your account exists. To delete your account and everything linked to it, email the address on the contact page from the Google address you signed in with; deletion completes within 30 days. You can delete history and library items, and revoke API keys, from the app at any time.

Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, UK or California you may request access to, correction of, export of, or deletion of your data, and you may object to processing, by contacting us. We do not sell personal data.

Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes.
